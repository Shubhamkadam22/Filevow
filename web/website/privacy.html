Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

The short version: your files, their fingerprints, their backup copies and your history stay on your own computer. To give you an account, FileVow stores your e-mail address and sign-in dates online, and, unless you turn it off, one small "used today" marker per day so we know how many people use FileVow. That's all. No file names, no file contents, no ads, no tracking.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers the FileVow desktop app, your FileVow account and this website. FileVow is published by the FileVow team ("we"), who decide how the account data described below is used.

2. What is stored online (your account)

InformationWhyWhere
Your e-mail address and an account IDTo create your account and sign you inSupabase (our sign-in provider)
When the account was created and when you last signed inSecurity, and counting how many people use FileVowSupabase
One record per day you use the app: the date, the FileVow version and the Windows versionCounting active users and knowing which versions are still in useSupabase. You can turn this off in Settings.

You sign in with a one-time code that our e-mail provider sends to your address. We never see or store a password: there isn't one. The username you choose is stored only on your computer.

3. What stays on your computer

InformationWhyWhere it's kept
The paths, fingerprints, sizes, timestamps, owners and permissions of files you protectTo notice when they changeFileVow's database
Encrypted backup copies of the files you protectTo show differences and restore filesFileVow's vault folder, encrypted with AES-256-GCM
Your signing key, encrypted with your passphraseTo seal fingerprints and approvalsFileVow's keys folder
Audit log: sign-ins (including the local IP address), checks and decisions with your reasonsSo you can see what happened and prove nothing was tampered withFileVow's database
Your username, a copy of your e-mail address and an encrypted sign-in tokenTo keep you signed in on this computer and send the daily markerFileVow's database, the token encrypted

By default this is the %LOCALAPPDATA%\FileVow folder of your Windows account. FileVow's own encryption keys are kept in the Windows Credential Manager. None of this is sent to us.

4. Service providers

  • Supabase hosts the account data above on our behalf.
  • Our e-mail provider delivers sign-in codes.
  • Vercel hosts this website; GitHub hosts the installer downloads. Both may keep standard access logs.

We don't sell your information or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

5. Notifications you set up

If you add a notification channel (e-mail, Slack, Microsoft Teams or a webhook), FileVow sends alert messages to the service you chose. They contain the alert title, severity, file path and time, never file contents, and go straight from your computer to that service, not through us.

6. Other people on the same computer

Each FileVow account has a private workspace. Other accounts, including the administrator account, can't see your protected files, changes, alerts or audit log in the app. Anyone with full administrator rights on Windows itself can technically access files on that computer, which is true of any desktop software.

7. This website

This website is a set of static pages. It doesn't use cookies, analytics or third-party trackers, and it doesn't ask for any personal information.

8. How long information is kept

Account data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your e-mail address, account ID and daily records are deleted from Supabase straight away. Data on your computer stays until you delete it: uninstalling FileVow and removing its data folder deletes everything it stored there.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off the daily marker: Settings → Count me as an active user.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete my account. This deletes your online data immediately.
  • See or correct your data: your e-mail address is shown in Settings; contact us for a copy of everything we hold about you.
  • Choose exactly which folders and files FileVow watches, and stop at any time.

Depending on where you live, data-protection laws (such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the EU GDPR) give you further rights, including to withdraw consent and to complain to a regulator. We'll respond to requests within 30 days.

10. Security

Account data is protected by row-level security, so each signed-in account can only write its own records and can't read anyone else's. Connections use HTTPS. Sensitive actions in the app need your signing-key passphrase, which never leaves your computer.

11. Children

FileVow isn't directed at children under 18, and we don't knowingly create accounts for them.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and describe significant changes in the release notes of the next version.

13. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy? Contact the FileVow team at privacy@filevow.app.
