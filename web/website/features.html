Features

Everything FileVow does for you

Detection, review, recovery and a trustworthy history, in one quiet app that runs on your own PC.

Detection

Real-time watching

Files are re-checked within seconds of being edited, renamed, deleted or added.

Full check every 15 minutes

A complete pass over everything you protect, in case a change was missed, plus Verify now whenever you like.

Content, permission and owner changes

Besides edits, FileVow notices when a file's Windows permissions or owner change.

New and deleted files

Files that appear in a protected folder, or disappear from it, are reported too.

Monitoring-gap warnings

If FileVow was switched off for a while, it tells you when it starts again, because something could have changed in between.

Smart exclusions

Skip files that change all the time, such as *.log, *.tmp or Office lock files.

Review and recovery

Side-by-side comparison

See the approved version next to what's on disk now, with removed and added lines highlighted.

Accept

Approve an expected change. The new version is fingerprinted, sealed with your key and backed up.

Restore

Put back the last approved version from the encrypted backup copy in one click.

Quarantine

Remove an unexpected new file after keeping an encrypted evidence copy.

Reasons for every decision

Each decision records why it was made, so you can look back later.

Severity levels

Mark folders as critical, high, medium or low to decide how loudly changes are reported.

Security

Personal signing key

Your RSA-3072 signing key is created on your PC and encrypted with a passphrase only you know.

Encrypted backup vault

Backup copies are compressed and encrypted with AES-256-GCM, a modern authenticated cipher.

Windows Credential Manager

FileVow's own secrets are stored in the Windows credential store, not in plain files.

Two-step verification

Protect your account with a 6-digit code from an authenticator app, plus recovery codes.

Confirm sensitive actions

Accepting, restoring and removing files always asks for your password (and code) again.

Lockout and safe sessions

Repeated wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes; sessions expire automatically.

History and alerts

Tamper-evident audit log

Every sign-in, check and decision is chained together; editing or deleting an entry breaks the chain visibly.

Signed verification reports

Each check produces a report sealed by FileVow, so the history itself can't be faked.

Alerts and escalation

Unacknowledged critical alerts are escalated after 15 minutes, high ones after an hour.

Notification channels

Windows notifications, email, Slack, Microsoft Teams or your own webhook. Alerts never include file contents.

Export

Download your audit log as CSV whenever you need it.

Private workspaces

Every account on the PC has its own folders, key, changes and history, invisible to the others.

Everyday use

Feels like a Windows app

Opens in its own window with a familiar Windows 11 look, light and dark themes.

Browse to choose files

Pick folders or single files from Desktop, Documents, Downloads or any drive.

Get-started checklist

A short guide on first launch walks you through your key and your first folder.

Starts with Windows

Optionally keep monitoring in the background whenever you sign in.

Search and shortcuts

Ctrl+K to find any file or command; keyboard shortcuts for every review decision.

Built-in help

Every feature is explained in plain language inside the app.

Ready to protect your files?

Free for personal use. Installs in a minute.
