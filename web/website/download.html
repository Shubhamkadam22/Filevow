System requirements

  • Operating system Windows 10 (21H2 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit
  • Browser engine Microsoft Edge (built into Windows) shows the app window
  • Disk space About 150 MB, plus room for encrypted backup copies of what you protect
  • Memory 4 GB RAM or more
  • Permissions Installs for your Windows account only; no administrator rights needed

Install

  1. Run FileVow-Setup.exe from your Downloads folder. If Windows SmartScreen says it doesn't recognise the app yet, choose More info and check the publisher before choosing Run anyway.
  2. Choose whether FileVow should start with Windows (recommended, so monitoring never stops).
  3. Open FileVow from the Start menu. It opens in its own window.

Your first five minutes

  1. Create your account. The first account on a computer becomes its administrator.
  2. Create your signing key from the Get started checklist and choose a passphrase you'll remember.
  3. Click Add folder or file, then Browse… and choose what to protect.
  4. Download a backup of your signing key (Settings) and keep it somewhere safe.
  5. Turn on two-step verification and, if you like, email or Windows notifications.

The Help page inside the app explains every feature.

Check your download

To make sure the installer wasn't altered on its way to you, compare its fingerprint with the one in the checksum file. In PowerShell:

Get-FileHash $HOME\Downloads\FileVow-Setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256

Uninstall

Use Settings → Apps → Installed apps → FileVow → Uninstall. Your protected files are never touched. FileVow's own data (records and encrypted backups) stays in %LOCALAPPDATA%\FileVow until you delete that folder.
